Scheduled sessions

Locus automatically starts a focus session on the days and time you choose. Set a task once and it runs on repeat — e.g. "Math HW" every Mon & Thu at 3:00 PM.

Lockdown

A commitment device. During the window Locus force-runs a focus session on one of the tasks you pick and won't let you pause, end, or disable it. It can also run at its own harshness, timer and allowlist for the duration, then hand your normal settings back. Once a window starts you're in until it ends — editing mid-window won't release you, and the settings stay frozen until it does. The only escape is disabling the extension.

iCal feeds

Paste any public or "secret" iCal/.ics URL — Google Calendar's secret address, iCloud, Outlook, Schoology, Canvas. Locus refreshes every 30 minutes and shows the next 14 days on the Start pane.
