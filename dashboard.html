About Locus

Locus is a Manifest V3 Chrome extension. You define activities with a domain whitelist; while a session is active, every other site is blocked. An AI gatekeeper decides whether your reasons for breaking focus are plausible.

Version 0.3 · No tracking, no telemetry. All analytics are local.

  • Per-session task — passed into AI prompts as context.
  • Smart re-blocking via title-based drift detection.
  • Tightened denial UX (denial locks per tab).
  • iCal calendar import (Google secret address, iCloud, Outlook, Schoology, Canvas).
  • Local analytics: 14-day series, streaks, top blocked domains.
